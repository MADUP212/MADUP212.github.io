Marc-Antoine S. Dupuis

Master's student in Political Science at Laval University, student researcher at the Center for Public Policy Analysis, and reserve soldier in the logistics branch of the Canadian Armed Forces.

Both as a soldier and student researcher, I focus on Canadian and Quebec public policies, as well as social and economic issues. My current research projects analyze Canadian census data and social stratification.
