Project

Since 2026 · prof-datagotchi.com

Co-founder of Prof. Datagotchi

Prof. Datagotchi is a robot professor who answers questions, in character, about what Quebec party leaders have said ahead of the 2026 general election. He does not guess: he quotes. Every answer rests on a verifiable corpus of interviews, press scrums, platforms and press releases, and the professor names his source rather than filling a gap.

Built at the Centre d'analyse des politiques publiques and the CLESSN at Université Laval, among the Datagotchi projects, he shares a classroom with the Vitrine démocratique and the Défi Datagotchi. I am one of its co-founders, and it went online in beta for the 2026 campaign.

  • 285 interviews transcribed
  • 5 parties, 6 spokespeople
  • Platforms, press releases, candidate profiles

Current research

CLESSN · CAPP · since 2026

Social and linguistic stratification in Quebec and Canada

My research project looks at social and linguistic stratification in Quebec and Canada: how language and social position — income, occupation, education — overlap, and how that overlap shifts from one census to the next.

I carry it out as a student researcher at the Chaire de leadership en enseignement des sciences sociales numériques (CLESSN) and the Centre d'analyse des politiques publiques (CAPP) at Université Laval, since 2026, using Canadian census data.
