About

Marc-Antoine S. Dupuis is a master’s student in political science at Université Laval and a student researcher at the CLESSN and the Centre d’analyse des politiques publiques. He works on social class and language in Quebec, the sociodemographic bases of vote choice, the fulfillment of election pledges, and military data. He has served in the Canadian Armed Forces Reserve since 2021.
